About

I’m Inês Santos Silva, a Portuguese entrepreneur based in Portugal. I work on women’s health, the business of health and longevity, building companies and funding the research that has been missing.

What I do

  • Co-founder & COO, TejoMed - Portugal’s first membership-based family medicine clinic, built around prevention and long-term doctor-patient relationships.
  • Core team, AthenaDAO - a funding platform backing early-stage biotech in women’s health and ovarian aging.
  • Co-founder, Portuguese Women in Tech - where I organize the Women & Wealth and Women & Leadership conferences.
  • Board of advisors, FemTech Portugal, and non-executive board member at Almedina, a Portuguese publisher.

Background

I’m an alumna of the Singularity University Graduate Studies Program (2013) and the THNK School of Creative Leadership (2014), and a Marshall Memorial Fellow (2019) of the German Marshall Fund.

On this site

My argument for why women’s health is a major opportunity, my view on AI and the future of work, my articles and podcasts, short dispatches, speaking, salon dinners and my bookshelf. To get in touch, see Contact.
